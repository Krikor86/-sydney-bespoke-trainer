Frequently Asked Questions

PERSONAL TRAINING IN SYDNEY CBD.

Where is Sydney Bespoke Trainer located?

Training is based at 35 Clarence Street, Sydney NSW 2000, close to Wynyard and easily accessible throughout Sydney CBD.

Who is the coaching suitable for?

Coaching is tailored to your goals, training experience and weekly schedule, whether you are beginning, returning to training or seeking more advanced results.

Can you help with both muscle gain and fat loss?

Yes. Coaching can be structured for muscle and strength development, fat loss, body recomposition, movement quality and long-term health according to your individual needs.

Will I train directly with Greg?

Yes. You work directly with Greg Kafalian in every booked one-to-one session rather than being passed between different trainers.

What happens during the free consultation?

You discuss your goals, schedule, training history and any obstacles that have limited your progress. This determines whether the coaching approach is the right fit and what your next step should be.

How much does personal training cost?

Sessions begin at $125 for a 45-minute one-to-one appointment, with reduced per-session pricing available in training packs. Your consultation can identify the most suitable option.
