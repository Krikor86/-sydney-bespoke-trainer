Questions

BEFORE YOU
BOOK.

The things people usually ask before their first consultation. Anything else, ask on the day — there's no pressure and no sales pitch.

I've never trained with a coach before. Is this for me?

Yes. Most clients start with little or no structured training history. The first session is a free consultation, and your program is built from your starting point — not a template.

I have an injury or a niggle. Can I still train?

In most cases, yes. Every program starts with a movement and technique assessment, and exercise selection is adjusted around any injuries so you keep progressing safely.

How long are sessions and where do we train?

Sessions are 45 minutes, one-on-one, at 35 Clarence St in Sydney CBD.

How many sessions a week do I need?

That depends on your goal and schedule. Two to three sessions a week suits most clients, and it's one of the first things we work out at your consultation.

What happens after I enquire?

I'll be in touch within 24 hours to set up your free consultation. We'll talk through your goals, training history, injuries and schedule, then agree on the right next step. No obligation either way.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Train session by session or buy a pack of 10 or 20. There are no contracts or hidden fees.
