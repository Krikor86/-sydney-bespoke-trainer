Frequently Asked Questions

TRAINING AFTER 40.

Is it too late to build muscle after 40?

No. Adults can continue building muscle and strength after 40 with progressive resistance training, suitable nutrition and enough recovery. Your starting point, training history and health are considered when the program is designed.

Do I need previous gym experience?

No. Coaching is adapted to your current experience, from complete beginners to experienced lifters who want more precise programming and technique feedback.

Can training be adapted around old injuries or limitations?

Exercise selection, range of motion, loading and progression can be adjusted around your current capabilities. Medical clearance may be requested where appropriate.

How much does personal training cost?

Sessions begin at $125 for a 45-minute one-to-one appointment, with reduced per-session pricing available in training packs.

Where does the training take place?

Sessions are held at 35 Clarence Street in Sydney CBD, close to Wynyard and Barangaroo.
