Questions

Training
After 40.

Is it too late to begin strength training after 40?

No. Your program begins from your current level and progresses according to your experience, capabilities and recovery.

Do I need previous gym experience?

No. Coaching is adapted for beginners, people returning after time away and experienced lifters wanting a more precise plan.

Can training be adapted around old injuries?

Exercise selection, range of motion, loading and progression can be adjusted to your present capabilities. Medical clearance may be requested where appropriate.

Where does the training take place?

Sessions are held at 35 Clarence Street in Sydney CBD.

Start With A Clear Plan.

Discuss your goals, training history and schedule with Greg. There is no lock-in contract or obligation.
